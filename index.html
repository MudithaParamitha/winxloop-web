Our Story

Crafted with hands,
guided by feeling.

Winxloop was born from a belief that the objects around us should feel intentional. Each hanging charm and clay tray is shaped by hand, unhurried, in a small studio in Bandung.

We work with natural stones — fluorite, quartz, and river-washed beads — because nature already knows how to be beautiful. We just listen, and follow.

Our Story

Made by
hands,

Winxloop is a small handmade brand rooted in the idea that the things around you should feel meaningful. Every piece is shaped slowly, with care — in a quiet studio in Bandung, where creativity and nature meet.

What We Believe

The values behind
every piece.

01

Slow Making

We don't rush. Each piece takes the time it needs. That patience becomes part of the object — you can feel it when you hold it.

02

Natural First

Fluorite, shells, wood, clay. We choose materials that come from the earth, because they carry a calm that synthetic things simply can't replicate.

03

Yours, Truly

Customization isn't just a feature — it's our whole philosophy. Tell us who you are, and we'll make something that feels like it was born for you.
